Adjust your files

This website is created for those who want to easily and safely make adjustments to their PDFs. Primarily you can reorder, remove or add pages to a PDF. You can also merge multiple PDFs into one.

💸

Without payment

It costs nothing to use this website. We also have no annoying pop-up ads or require ad breaks for you to proceed. You may still see some ads but we want to design them to be as unobtrusive as possible. This makes this website perfect for students or others who may not want to pay to adjust small things in their PDFs.

We always want to improve!

We want it to be smooth and easy for you as a user. If you have any questions, thoughts or suggestions for improvement, don't hesitate to get in touch!
