Privacy Policy

Your privacy
matters to us

Last updated: May 2026

What data do we collect?

The only data we collect right now is the traffic going to and from our website. Currently, we are only logging who is accessing our server and how frequently.

At the moment PDF Cleaner processes all files locally in your browser — no files are sent to any server. This can change in the future with the introduction of cookies or other changes to the website. Please note! This can change in the future, but we are never planning to collect the data or information inside your pdf files.

How is the data used?

The information about who uses the website and how often is recorded in a log file that is cleared as needed. We monitor this traffic to gain insights that help us improve the website's performance and user experience.

Cookies

Currently we do not have or use cookies.
